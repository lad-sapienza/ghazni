<p>At Tapa Sardar many small-scale architectural elements &ndash; either in the form of profiled reliefs or in the round &ndash; have been found. All of them, however, were fragmentary and separated from their original setting. They include miniature pillars for wall decoration, small free-standing columns mainly composed of <em>āmalaka</em> elements, and miniature <em>stūpas</em>. Some samples are currently under study and hypotheses are being tested about their contextual use.&nbsp;</p>
