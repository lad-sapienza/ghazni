<p>Basins, most often sculpted in a square shape; the inner reservoir is often polylobed and inscribed in a hexagon or octagon. Some are carved in bas relief. Some have a drainage hole.</p>
<p>Artifacts were found in the Ghazni area or in the Rawza and Kabul museums in the years 1957-1966, or were purchased in Ghazni in the same years, or were excavated in the Ghaznavid palace and in the house of luster wares; other were first recorded in the Ghazni store-rooms in 2002.&nbsp;</p>
