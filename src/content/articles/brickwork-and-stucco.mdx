<p>The combination of brickwork and stucco occurs in panels and columns made of edgewise laid bricks composing a geometric pattern with stucco tiles inserted into. Please note that in some cases the tiles are made of brick as well; it is still unclear whether they represent a replacement of stucco tiles or not. Seventeen different geometric patterns have been identified so far.&nbsp;</p>
<p>For items made of a layer of decorated stucco bedded on a baked brick base, see the <a href="/islamic/finds/stucco">Stucco section</a>.</p>
