<p>Small metal objects, and especially coins, from the excavations of Tapa Sardar disappeared from the storerooms in Ghazni during the years of the civil war.</p>
<p>However, the MAIA had preserved at the Centro Scavi of IsMEO/IsIAO in Rome the casts of the coins, which are presently being studied by Michael Alram, Director of the Coin Collection of the Kunsthistorisches Museum Wien.</p>
