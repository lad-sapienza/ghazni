<p>Friezes were probably arranged in horizontal series composing superimposed registers. All come from the Ghaznavid Palace. The only identified type is with knotted pseudo-epigraphic motif and comes from the central area-west side, the central courtyard, the south-western area (private apartments).&nbsp;</p>
