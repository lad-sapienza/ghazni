<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 28b). The decoration is composed of a double sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are plain inside and alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions of the complete panel included in this sub-type are unknown, but the height of the larger fragment with known dimensions is 53 cm, its thickness 30.5 cm.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area (the surveys occurred in the late 1950s-1970s).</p>
<p>Type 28b differs from dadoes type 28a for the vegetal motifs.</p>
