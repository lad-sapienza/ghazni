<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 29). The decoration is composed of a double sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are plain inside and alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis; they also alternate in the lower and the upper course.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area between 1957 and 1958.</p>
