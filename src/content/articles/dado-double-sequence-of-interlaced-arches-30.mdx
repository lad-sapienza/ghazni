<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 30). The decoration is composed of two horizontal sections: the upper section consists of an epigraphic band in cursive script, the lower section of a double sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are plain inside and alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis; they also alternate in the lower and the upper course.</p>
<p>The height of the larger panel is 64.5 cm (incomplete); its width varies from 33.5 cm (incomplete) to 46.5 cm (complete) and 50 cm (incomplete); its thickness is unknown.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area (the surveys occurred in the late 1950s-1970s).</p>
