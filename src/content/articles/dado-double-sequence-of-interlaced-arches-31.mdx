<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 31). The decoration is composed of one section with a double sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches, which enclose an incised vegetal motif, alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis; they are positioned one on top of the other.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>One panel has incised marks that are related to the carvers&rsquo; practice; it might be a location mark.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area 1957.</p>
