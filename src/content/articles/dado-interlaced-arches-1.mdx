<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 1). This type is represented by one specimen.<br />The decoration is composed of one section with a sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are plain inside and alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was first recorded in the Ghazni new storerooms in 2002.</p>
<p>This type might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
