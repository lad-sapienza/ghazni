<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 19a). This type is represented by a single fragmentary specimen. The existing decoration is composed of two horizontal sections: the upper section consists of a narrow beaded band, the lower section of a sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are set against a vegetal pattern of interlaced half-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was first recorded in Ghazni in 2002.</p>
<p>Type 19a might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
