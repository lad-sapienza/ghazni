<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 3). This type is represented by one specimen.<br />The existing decoration is composed of four horizontal sections: the upper section and lowermost sections are not preserved; the second consists of a beaded band, the third section of a trilobated arch ending in a vegetal motif. The arch is plain inside. A lateral plain stripe constitutes the panel&rsquo;s left frame. The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was first recorded in the Ghazni new storerooms in 2002.</p>
<p>This type might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
