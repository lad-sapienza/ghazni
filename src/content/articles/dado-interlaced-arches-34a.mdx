<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 34a). The existing decoration is composed of a (single or double) sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches are plain inside and alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifacts were first recorded in the Rawza Museum in Ghazni in the late 1950s-1960s.</p>
<p>Type 34a differs from dado type 34b for the vegetal motifs.</p>
