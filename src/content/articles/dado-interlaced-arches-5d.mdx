<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 5d). This type is represented by one specimen.<br />The decoration is composed of one section with a sequence of interlaced trilobated arches alternating with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis. The arches enclose an incised vegetal motif.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was first recorded in the Ghazni new storerooms in 2002.</p>
<p>This type might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling. It differs from dadoes types 5a, 5b and 5c for the vegetal motifs.</p>
