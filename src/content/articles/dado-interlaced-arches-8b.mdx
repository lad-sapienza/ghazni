<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 8b). This type is represented by one fragmentary specimen. The existing decoration is composed of one section with a sequence of interlaced trilobated arches. The arches, which enclose an incised vegetal motif, alternate with vertical motifs created by split-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was first recorded in the Rawza Museum in Ghazni in 1957.</p>
<p>Type 8b differs from dado type 8a for the vegetal motifs.</p>
