<p>Rectangular panels carved in bas-relief composing a dado. The decoration is typically composed of three horizontal sections, with an inscription in the upper band and with a sequence of interlaced trefoil arches or of Persian horseshoe arches in the mid section. The inscription include a Persian poem, or Arabic benedictory sentences.</p>
<p>Some of the panels have incised marks that are related to the carvers&rsquo; practice; they might be location marks.</p>
<p>The artifacts were excavated in the Ghaznavid palace, or found re-employed in the Ghazni area, or recorded in the Rawza Museum and in the Kabul National Museum in the years 1957-1966.</p>
