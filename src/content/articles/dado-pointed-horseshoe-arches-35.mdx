<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 35). This type is represented by one specimen. The decoration is composed of two horizontal sections: the upper section consist of a narrow beaded band, the lower section of a sequence of pointed horseshoe arches supported by columns. The arches enclose a vegetal motifs created by half-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifact was purchased in Ghazni area in 1958.</p>
<p>Type 35 might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
