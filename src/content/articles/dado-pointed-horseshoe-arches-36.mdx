<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 36). The decoration is composed of three horizontal sections: the upper section consist of a narrow beaded band alternating with vegetal elements, the middle section of a continuous sequence of pointed horseshoe arches. The arches enclose and are alternating with vegetal motifs created by half-palmettes and fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The dimensions are unknown.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area in 1957.</p>
<p>Type 36 might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
