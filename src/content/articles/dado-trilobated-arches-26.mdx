<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 26). This type is represented by one fragmentary specimen. The existing decoration is composed of a sequence of trilobated arches enclosing and alternating with vegetal motifs.</p>
<p>The height of the panel is 17 cm, its width 14 cm (both incomplete); its thickness is 4 cm.</p>
<p>The artifact was excavated in the Ghaznavid palace (the excavation occurred in the late 1950s-1960s).</p>
<p>Type 26 might have been used as an architectural dado or as a tomb paneling.</p>
