<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 41). This type is represented by one specimen.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of two horizontal sections: the upper section consists of a figurative scene, the lower section of a sequence of fleur-de-lis with points down. In the scene two affronted elephants hold together a vegetal element with their trunks.</p>
<p>The height of the panel is 33 cm, its width is 60 cm; its thickness is 8.5 cm.</p>
<p>The artifact was purchased in Ghazni area in the late 1950s-1960s.</p>
<p>It differs from type 42 for the lower section.</p>
