<p>Dado carved in bas-relief (type 43).</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of two horizontal sections separated by a narrow beaded band: the upper section consists of a cursive epigraphic band, the lower section of a figurative scene. <br />The height of the only panel with known dimension is 28 cm, its width is 38 cm; its thickness is unknown.</p>
<p>One panel has incised marks that are related to the carvers&rsquo; practice; it might be a location mark.</p>
<p>The artifact was found re-employed in the Ghazni area; another was first recorded in the Rawza Museum in Ghazni (the surveys occurred in the late 1950s-1970s).</p>
