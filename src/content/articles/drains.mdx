<p>Drains, mostly sculpted in the shape of animal-heads.</p>
<p>Artifacts were found in the Ghazni area or in the Rawza and Kabul museums in the years 1957-1966, or were purchased in Ghazni in the same years; other were first recorded in the Kabul store-rooms in 2004.</p>
