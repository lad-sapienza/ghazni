<p>Floor slabs, rectangular, with slightly converging sides. Some bear incised motifs related to the stonemasons&rsquo; work.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area in the late 1950s-1960s or excavated in the Ghaznavid palace.</p>
