<p>Frames, composed by rectangular and L-shaped panels, carved in bas-relief with an inscribed band, sometimes with a second register with vegetal motifs. Other examples, all with&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 1.42857143;">animal motifs,&nbsp;</span><span style="line-height: 1.42857143;">are probably mouldings. The inscriptions often include honorific titles; one building text and one religious invocation are also attested.</span></p>
<p>Most artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area in 1957 and 1958, or excavated in the Ghaznavid palace; some were recorded in the Ghazni and Kabul storerooms in 2002 and 2004.</p>
