<h4>A private residence of the medieval period</h4>
<div>Brought to light during excavations in the years 1957, this private residence is named after the almost intact assemblage of <a href="/islamic/finds/lustrewares">lustre-painted ceramics</a> found in it. The site is completely dismantled today.</div>
<p>The excavators attributed it to the Ghurid period, suggesting that its abandonment dates back to the Mongol conquest of Ghazni in 1221.&nbsp;</p>
