<p><strong><span style="line-height: 1.1;">2016, 3rd-4th November</span></strong></p>
<p>Stuttgart (Germany), international workshop <em>Khurasan: the Land of the Rising Sun</em> convened by L. Korn, U. Franke and A. Kraemer at the Linden-Museum.</p>
<p>Viola Allegranzi presented a paper entitled "The Medieval Urbanism of Ghazni (Afghanistan), A Cross-reading of Textual and Material Evidence, 10th-12th centuries".</p>
<p>Valentina Laviola gave a talk entitled "Some Islamic Buckets from Ghazni and Khurasan. A Comparative Perspective".<em style="line-height: 1.42857143;">&nbsp;</em></p>
