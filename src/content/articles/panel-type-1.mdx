<p>Rectangular panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a kufic epigraphic band and a main section with a hexagons modular pattern which generates cross-shaped elements and lozenges. Different vegetal motifs fill the geometrical elements.</p>
<p>The artifact was found re-employed in a ziyara of the Ghazni area in 1957.</p>
