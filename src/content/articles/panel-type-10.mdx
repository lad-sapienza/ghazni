<p>Panels (prob. rectangular) composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of an exterior epigraphic frame, and of a main rectangular (prob.) section with whirls modular pattern. The epigraphic frame is in Kufic script; a vase-shaped element might be inserted in the line, interrupting the sequence of the words. The whirls modular pattern consists of thin whirls departing each from a small circle, and having four thin and elongated wings (each decorated by three small feather-like elements), whose ends are interlaced and converge two by two towards opposing fleur-de-lis in the contour panel.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area late 1950s-1960s.</p>
