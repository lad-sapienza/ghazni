<p>Rectangular panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a frame (epigraphic in the upper and lateral sides, vegetal in the lower one), and of main rectangular section with a vegetal pattern. The epigraphic frame is in kufic script with; palmettes are set in the corners, interrupting the sequence of the words. The vegetal frame shows a sequence of fleur-de-lis.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area late 1950s-1960s.</p>
