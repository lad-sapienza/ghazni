<p>Rectangular panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of an epigraphic band in cursive script, and a central section with large palmettes. Rectangular hole for brackets in the front face.</p>
<p>The only known artifact included in this type was purchased at Ghazni in late 1950s.</p>
