<p>Rectangular panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a lateral epigraphic band in cursive script, and of a rectangular main section with a vegetal pattern (scrolls, palmettes and fleur-de-lis).</p>
<p>The only known artifact included in this type was found in the Ghazni area in 1957.</p>
