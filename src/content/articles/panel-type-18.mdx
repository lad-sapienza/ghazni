<p>Rectangular panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a framing epigraphic band in cursive script on the two lateral sides, of an epigraphic cartouche in cursive script, and a lower field with a geometric pattern.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area in 1966.</p>
