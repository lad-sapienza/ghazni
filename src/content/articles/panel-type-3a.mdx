<p>Rectangular and L-shaped panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a lateral kufic epigraphic frame and a main section with an octagons modular pattern. A fleur-de-lis is inserted in every corner of the epigraphic band, interrupting the sequence of the words.</p>
<p>The octagons modular pattern shows: octagons with double border, filled by incised vegetal motifs; the contour panels show opposing pairs of trilobated and interlaced palmettes containing a fleur-de-lis, and converging in groups of four towards a decorative knot inscribed in a octagon pattern.</p>
<p>Three artifacts were excavated in the Ghaznavid palace; others were found re-employed in the Ghazni area in 1957.</p>
