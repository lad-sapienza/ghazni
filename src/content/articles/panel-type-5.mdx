<p>Rectangular and L-shaped panels composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a kufic epigraphic band, of a main section with an octagons modular pattern and of a lower band of rolled up scrolls. The epigraphic band frames the main section; a double fleur-de-lis is inserted in every corner of the band, interrupting the sequence of the words. The octagons modular pattern, alternating horizontal sequences of smaller and larger octagons, generates four-pointed stars; different vegetal motifs fill the geometrical elements.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in the Ghazni area (late 1950s-1970s).</p>
