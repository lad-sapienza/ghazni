<p>Panels (prob. rectangular) composing a high facing.</p>
<p>As concerns the existing decoration, it shows an octagons modular pattern, generating cross-shaped elements. Palmettes, scrolls and fleur-de-lis are carved inside the octagons, while converging fleur-de-lis inside the cross-shaped elements.</p>
<p>The artifacts were found re-employed in a ziyara of the Ghazni area in 1957 and 1958.</p>
