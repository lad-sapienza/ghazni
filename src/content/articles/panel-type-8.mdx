<p>Panels (prob. rectangular) composing a high facing.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of a modular pattern of squares, each filled with different vegetal or geometric motifs.</p>
<p>The only known artifact included in this type was excavated in the Ghaznavid palace in the late 1950s-1960s.</p>
