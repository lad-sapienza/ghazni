<p>Panels with geometric pattern and brick inserts features brick inserts whose shape is different from that of their slots (for instance, flower-shaped inserts occupy hexagonal slots). This makes them differ from the Brick panels with stucco tiles (see <a href="https://ghazni.bdus.cloud/islamic/finds/brickwork-and-stucco" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Brickwork and stucco</a>), where stucco tiles fit precisely in the slots created by the brick geometric pattern.</p>
