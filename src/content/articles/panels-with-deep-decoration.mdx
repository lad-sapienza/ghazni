<p>Panels with deep-carved decoration show two main groups: a vegetal one and a second composed by vegetal elements in a geometric pattern. A few come from the Ghaznavid Palace, about 68 have unknown provenance. The latter resemble panel decorating the minaret of Bahramshah in Ghazni.</p>
