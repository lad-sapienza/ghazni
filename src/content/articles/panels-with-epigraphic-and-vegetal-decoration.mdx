They usually feature a short Kufic inscription in the upper band and a vegetal pattern in the wider section below (the majority of inscriptions is not combined with the vegetal decoration; see <a href="/islamic/finds/brickwork/epigraphic-panels">Epigraphic panels and frames</a>.</p>
