<p>Panels with flat lateral pillar show a flat version of the decoration typical of composite pillars (see the category <a href="https://ghazni.bdus.cloud/islamic/finds/brickwork/bw_columns" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Pillars and columns</a>), featuring a base, a small column, and a capital (either vegetal or geometric).</p>
