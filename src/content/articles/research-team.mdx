<h4 class="text-center p-4 bg-gray">DIRECTORS</h4>
<div class="container">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Roberta Giunta (Islamic section)</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>rgiunta@unior.it</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Full Professor of Islamic Archaeology and Art History, and Islamic Epigraphy at the Dipartimento Asia, Africa e Mediterraneo of the University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo;. Since 1993 she carried out archaeological and epigraphic researches in many countries of the Near and Middle East. Since 2004, she is deputy director of the Italian Archaeological Mission in Afghanistan and responsible for the Islamic section. She is the author of several monographic studies and numerous papers published in national and international journals.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Anna Filigenzi (Buddhist section)</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>afiligenzi@unior.it</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Associate Professor in Indian Archaeology and Art History at the University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo;. She is the director of the Italian Archaeological Mission in Afghanistan since 2004, and a member of the Italian Archaeological Mission in Pakistan since 1984. She is a member of several scientific institutions, in Italy and abroad.</p>

<h4 class="text-center p-4 bg-gray">COORDINATORS ISLAMIC SECTION</h4>
<div class="container">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Valentina Laviola</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>vlaviola@unior.it</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Post-doctoral Research Fellow at the University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo; and Adjunct Professor of Islamic Art and Archaeology at the University of Bologna. She obtained her PhD in 2016 at the Ca&rsquo; Foscari University of Venice. Her studies are devoted to Islamic archaeology and material culture, with a focus on the production of medieval Eastern Iranian lands.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Martina Rugiadi</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>martina.rugiadi@metmuseum.org</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Associate Curator at The Metropolitan Museum of Art and co-director of the Towns of the Karakum (ToKa) archaeological project. She recently co-edited T<em>he Seljuqs and their Successors. Art, Culture and History</em> (Edinburgh University Press, 2020). Her research is situated at the intersection of archaeology and art history. Current interests include craft practices, spolia, Islamic art historiography.</p>
</div>

<h4 class="text-center p-4 bg-gray">RESEARCH MEMBERS</h4>
<div class="container">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Viola Allegranzi</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>viola.allegranzi@gmail.com</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Post-doctoral Research Fellow at the Institute of Iranian Studies of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (Vienna). In 2017, she obtained a joint PhD at the Universities Sorbonne Nouvelle and University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo;, with a dissertation focused on the Persian inscriptions from Ghazni. Her main fields of research are monumental epigraphy, Persian literature and historiography, and the material culture of Iran and Central Asia (8th-14th century).</p>
</div>
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Arturo Annucci</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>arturo94annucci@gmail.com</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Ph.D. student in Islamic Numismatics (University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo;). Since the MA, he has been focusing on the coinage of the Eastern Iranian territories, studying also at the M&uuml;nzkabinett and the Austrian Academy of Sciences (Vienna). He is part as numismatist in international projects and archaeological missions.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Agnese Fusaro</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>agnese.fusaro@gmail.com</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Archaeologist and ceramics specialist. She got a PhD in Islamic archaeology in 2014 at Sapienza University of Rome. She is a member of the ERAAUB team (University of Barcelona). She has participated and currently participates in many archaeological projects in Syria, Iran, Iraqi Kurdistan, Pakistan, Oman, Uzbekistan. Working experience in the museum field and in academic teaching.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h5 class="mt-4 font-weight-medium mb-0">Martina Massullo</h5>
<p class="subtitle font-weight-normal"><em>massullo.martina@gmail.com</em></p>
<p class="mt-3">Post-doctoral Research Fellow at the Centre de Recherche sur le Monde Iranien (Paris), she obtained a joint PhD at Aix-Marseille University and the University of Naples &ldquo;L&rsquo;Orientale&rdquo; in 2017, with a dissertation focused on Arabic funerary inscriptions from Ghazni. Her main fields of research are Islamic epigraphy and funerary architecture with a particular focus on Iran and Central Asia. Current interests include sacred shrines and devotional topography in late medieval and early modern Afghanistan.</p>
