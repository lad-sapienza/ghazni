<p>Panels in this section are made of a stucco layer bedded on a base of baked brick. For items with a combination of brick and stucco, see Brickwork and stucco. Panels and frames have been distinguished so far on the basis of size (frames are less wide). Since no find was found in situ it is difficult to assign them.<br />Frames are very fragmentary, but they are among the few finds still preserving the polychrome painted decoration.</p>
