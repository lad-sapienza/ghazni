<p>Screen&nbsp;carved in bas-relief and with openwork decoration (type 3). This type is represented by one specimen.</p>
<p>The screen is framed on the four sides by a Kufic epigraphic band, enclosing the openwork panel. This shows a geometrical pattern with alternating hexagons and six-pointed stars.</p>
<p>Marble brackets (possibly two) sculpted from the same marble block are in the upper side.<br />The artifact was found re-employed in the Ghazni area in 1957.</p>
