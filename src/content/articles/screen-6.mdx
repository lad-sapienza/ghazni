<p>Screen&nbsp;carved in bas-relief and with openwork decoration (type 6). This type is represented by one specimen. The existing decoration shows an openwork panel with hexagons framed, in the lower side by a band with a sequence of two interlaced and rolled up half-palmettes.<br />The artifact was purchased at Ghazni in 1960.</p>
