<p>Screen&nbsp;(with rounded top) carved in bas-relief on both sides (type 7). This type is represented by one specimen. On both sides the decoration is composed of one section.</p>
<p>Side A. The decoration shows a gradient lion.</p>
<p>Side B. The decoration shows a vegetal pattern of interlaced half-palmettes and fleur-de-lis. <br />The artifact was first recorded in the Kabul National Museum in 1958; it came from Ghazni.</p>
