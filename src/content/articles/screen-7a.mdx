<p>Screen&nbsp;(with rounded top) carved in bas-relief on both sides (type 7a). This type is represented by one specimen. Its interpretation as a transenna is based on the rounded top, which parallels screen type 7; however, the back is left plain.</p>
<p>The decoration is composed of one section with scrolls ending in a dragon and deer, probably part of a <em>waq waq</em> motif or as tails of a seated lion.</p>
<p>The artifact was purchased at Ghazni in 1958.</p>
