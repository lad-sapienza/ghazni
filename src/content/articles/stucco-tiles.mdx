<p>Stucco tiles have measures comprised between 12 and 15 cm. Some of them were found still inserted into panels, more than one hundred isolated. They show a variety of geometric shapes: definitions indicate regular polygons even though they are not always precise. Some are carved, some stamped (see for instance a series of hexagonal tiles painted in red). Many still preserved the original painting in good state. On the same type of tiles, the colour is usually uniform.&nbsp;</p>
