<p>Elements employed in the water systems, including basins, tanks, well curbs and drains. Some are carved in bas-relief, drains are sculpted in the shape of animal-heads.</p>
<p>Artifacts were found in the Ghazni area or in the Rawza and Kabul museums in the years 1957-1966, or were purchased in Ghazni in the same years, or were excavated in the Ghaznavid palace and in the house of luster wares; other were first recorded in the Kabul and in Ghazni store-rooms in 2002 and 2004.</p>
