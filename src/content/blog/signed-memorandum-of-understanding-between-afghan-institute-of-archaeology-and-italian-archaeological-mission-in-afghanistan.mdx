<p><strong>November 2014</strong></p>
<p>A Memorandum of Understanding between the Director of the Afghan Institute of Archaeology, Mr. Abdul Qadir Temori, the Field Director Mr. Zafar Paiman,&nbsp; and the Director of the Italian Archaeological Mission in Afghanistan, Mrs. Anna Filigenzi, set forth the intentions for increased collaboration and cooperation.</p>
<p>The parties agreed to undertake specific joint projects aimed at expanding knowledge on the Afghan cultural history of the 1<sup>st</sup> millennium CE. Common actions are envisaged in order to promote field activities as well as theoretical studies, according to a flexible agenda that will be adapted to circumstances.</p>
<p>As a first step, a programme targeted at cross-comparison of data and mutual support for field activities (with particular regard to Tapa Sardar, Tepe Narenj and Qol-e Tut) has been established.&nbsp;</p>
